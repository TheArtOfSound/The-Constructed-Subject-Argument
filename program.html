Program map

Three tracks, one identification problem.

The central problem is not whether a system can produce a report. It is whether observed reports and behavior identify the mechanism or subject-level property being claimed.

01

Constructed Subject Argument

Separate artificial origin from present subjectivity.

The theory distinguishes historical authenticity, represented content, causal organization, and possible experience. It rejects both automatic inclusion and automatic exclusion.

  • Twenty-one chapter architecture
  • Claims and originality ledgers
  • Rival theories and falsifiers
  • Subject–Report Identification framing

02

EGC 2.0

Measure human judgments without treating them as mechanism readouts.

EGC 2.0 is a paired human-study architecture for studying how origin, continuity, mechanism, valence, and framing alter judgments while preserving underdetermination.

  • Paired estimands and sensitivity contracts
  • Rater calibration and anchor review
  • Lineage, runtime, and evidence closure
  • Explicit null and failure preservation

What has actually been built

A research stack, not one claim.

Each layer closes a different failure mode. None converts uncertainty into a declaration of consciousness, awareness, deception, or hidden intention.

A

Argument layer

Interactive book and epistemic ledgers

The public experience, chapter library, claims ledger, originality ledger, objections, literature map, and theory comparison framework make the philosophical synthesis inspectable.

B

Measurement layer

Paired EGC analysis and rater architecture

Estimands, pairing logic, sensitivity analysis, lineage requirements, calibration anchors, and machine-readable result contracts constrain what a future human study may claim.

C

Integrity layer

QEIB separation and provenance

Public development tasks, protected assessment, raw outputs, outcome families, and provenance are kept conceptually separate so one lane cannot impersonate another.

D

Evidence layer

Leakage, closure, and repository-native CI

Public artifacts are checked for forbidden disclosures and missing evidence references. Focused workflows preserve logs, exact tested commits, and failed states.

E

Operational layer

Synthetic cloud dry-run boundary

Operator assignment, independent review, Proton/AWS controls, Object Lock, CloudTrail, KMS separation, and twelve preflight gates are specified—but remain unassigned and unprovisioned.

Claim boundary

What the current program supports—and what it does not.

Supported

The public research architecture exists.

  • The theory, ledgers, experiment designs, analysis contracts, validators, fixtures, and evidence gates are public.
  • The current dry-run records are internally explicit about non-readiness.
  • Repository workflows have exercised focused engineering contracts.
  • Null, blocked, and failed states are treated as results to preserve.

Unresolved

The measurement process is not empirically validated.

  • No synthetic Proton/AWS dry run has been executed.
  • No accountable operator or independent reviewer is assigned.
  • No confirmatory human EGC result exists.
  • No benchmark result licenses an inference of hidden intention, awareness, deception, subjectivity, or consciousness.

The program is public enough to inspect—and incomplete enough to challenge.
