  1. 1

    The problem

    Systems can produce first-person language. That is easy to treat as proof of an inner subject.

    The stricter question is whether the report identifies a subject at all, which process is the subject, and whether the reporting process is the same as the reported one.

    Working name: Subject–Report Identification Problem.
