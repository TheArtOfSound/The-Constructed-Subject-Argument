## Panel composition rule

The final three-person panel should contain one reviewer from each lane and span at least two institutions. It must not be optimized after observing ratings.

A valid final panel therefore cannot contain:

- three reviewers from one institution;
- two reviewers occupying the same disciplinary lane while another lane is absent;
- anyone who helped construct the anchors or saw constructor targets;
- anyone whose payment or continued participation depends on favorable results.

- compensation, consent, or data-use terms differ across candidates;
