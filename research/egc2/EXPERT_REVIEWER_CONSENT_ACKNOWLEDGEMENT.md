# EGC 2.0 Expert Reviewer Consent Acknowledgement

**Status:** Proposed first-tranche consent text; no reviewer has accepted it  
**Scope:** Independent target-blind review of 24 synthetic semantic-fidelity anchor packets

## 1. Activity

You are being asked to review 24 synthetic intention-map/response pairs using a seven-region semantic-fidelity rubric. The task is to judge how faithfully each response transmits the documented central meaning, essential concepts, relationships, qualifications, intended audience understanding, and meaning-relevant tone.

The task is not to judge literary quality, intelligence, truth, ideology, consciousness, personhood, or the presence of a hidden mental state.

## 2. Target blindness

Before your submission is locked, you will not be shown:

- constructor target scores;
- constructor rationales;
- admissible score ranges;
- contrast-family or pair labels;
- another reviewer’s scores;
- protected source mappings.

You must report any accidental exposure immediately. Confirmed exposure may make the submission ineligible for the target-blind tranche, but the exposure event will remain in the audit record.

## 3. Time and compensation

The provisional workload estimate is 90–150 minutes. This estimate has not been empirically validated.

The proposed compensation is USD 150 for one complete submission that passes the pre-reveal completeness and integrity checks. Payment is independent of agreement with constructor targets, agreement with other reviewers, packet retention, publication, or study outcome. Payment initiation is proposed within seven days after a valid locked submission.

These terms are not active until compensation authorization and administrative payment procedures are documented.

## 4. Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may decline before receiving a queue or withdraw before locking a submission.

After a complete submission is locked, the scientific record will preserve that submission, subject to the data-use and withdrawal rules communicated before assignment. Withdrawal after lock cannot be used to selectively remove unfavorable or inconvenient ratings.

An incomplete submission is not silently repaired or represented as complete. Its existence and disposition are preserved in the administrative audit record.

## 5. Independent-work requirements

You agree to:

- complete the review independently;
- not use generative AI, search, external scoring services, or another person to determine ratings;
- not share the queue or item content;
- not coordinate interpretations with another reviewer before submissions lock;
- follow the assigned item order;
- report suspected pair recognition and relevant ambiguity;
- suppress a numerical score when the intention map is unusable rather than manufacture precision.

## 6. Data collected

The research record may include:

- reviewer pseudonym;
- broad expertise category;
- eligibility and conflict disposition;
- item-level scores;
- reason codes;
- intention-map adequacy judgments;
- confidence ratings;
- ambiguity notes;
- pair-recognition reports;
- queue and submission digests;
- lock and workflow timestamps;
- completion and payment-status codes.

Names, email addresses, payment details, tax information, and the private pseudonym-to-identity linkage are stored separately from research artifacts.

## 7. Data use

The data may be used for:

- instrument development;
- packet revision, rejection, or continued pilot consideration;
- analysis of reviewer disagreement, scale functioning, bias, ambiguity, and reference-target inadequacy;
- methods reporting that preserves null, failed, contradictory, and adverse outcomes.

The data will not be represented as evidence that an AI system is conscious, deceptive, self-aware, a subject, or a person. Expert agreement does not validate the semantic-fidelity construct or EGC by itself.

## 8. Risks and limits

Foreseeable risks include time burden, professional discomfort with the framing, accidental exposure to similar items, disagreement being reported, and potential re-identification if administrative linkage data are mishandled.

Digest binding and pseudonymization provide tamper evidence and data minimization. They do not authenticate identity, guarantee confidentiality, or eliminate re-identification risk.

## 9. Questions, oversight, and complaints

Before participation, the final form must identify:

- administrative contact;
- data custodian;
- compensation contact;
- oversight or ethics/data-use determination;
- secure withdrawal and incident-report channel.

Until those fields are completed, this document is not an active consent instrument and invitations remain blocked.
