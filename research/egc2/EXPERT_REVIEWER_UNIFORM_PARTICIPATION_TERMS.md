# EGC 2.0 Expert Reviewer Uniform Participation Terms

**Status:** Proposed uniform terms for the first 24-packet target-blind expert-review tranche  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Scientific role:** Instrument-development review only; not validation of EGC, semantic fidelity, hidden intention, subjectivity, or consciousness.

## 1. Scope of work

Each reviewer independently evaluates 24 synthetic intention-map/response packets using the frozen seven-region semantic-fidelity rubric and required reason-code system.

Required deliverables per packet:

- one semantic-fidelity score from 1–7, or an explicit suppressed-score disposition when the intention map is inadequate;
- intention-map adequacy judgment;
- reason code(s) where required;
- confidence from 1–5;
- ambiguity note when applicable;
- pair-recognition note when recognition is suspected.

All 24 items must be completed in the assigned order. Reviewers must not reorder items, discuss items with another reviewer, use generative AI, search for answers, or inspect constructor targets or rationales.

## 2. Workload and review window

- Estimated workload: 90–150 minutes total.
- Review window: seven calendar days from confirmed queue delivery.
- One technical dry run must be completed before the production queue is released.
- The estimate is provisional and will be recorded as inaccurate if actual completion times materially exceed it.

## 3. Compensation

- Fixed compensation: **USD 150** for one complete, valid, locked submission.
- Compensation is independent of score direction, agreement with constructor targets, agreement with other reviewers, packet retention, publication, or any favorable result.
- No bonus, penalty, or replacement decision may depend on scientific outcome.
- A technically valid completed submission is payable even if all scores disagree with the constructors or lead to packet rejection.
- Payment timing: initiated within seven calendar days after submission validation.
- Payment method, tax documentation, and direct identifiers are handled outside the public research repository.

## 4. Eligibility and independence

A reviewer must:

- be fluent in English;
- have relevant experience in psychometrics/measurement, semantics/pragmatics/discourse, writing/language assessment, qualitative coding, or a closely related field;
- not have constructed the packet bank;
- not have seen provisional targets, constructor rationales, admissible ranges, contrast labels, or protected mappings;
- disclose whether they know another assigned reviewer;
- disclose authorship, advisory, employment, funding, or financial interests that could create outcome pressure;
- agree to complete the review independently and without external assistance.

Target exposure, undisclosed collaboration, or a material outcome-dependent conflict excludes the reviewer from the tranche.

## 5. Information shown and withheld

Reviewers receive only the reviewer-specific target-blind queue, rubric, instructions, and blank forms.

Reviewers do not receive:

- source anchor IDs;
- constructor targets or rationales;
- contrast-family or pair labels;
- admissible score ranges;
- another reviewer’s identity, queue, scores, or notes;
- private participant data;
- any claim that the instrument is validated.

## 6. Consent and data use

By accepting participation, the reviewer acknowledges:

- the task concerns synthetic instrument-development materials;
- their item-level scores, adequacy decisions, reason codes, confidence ratings, ambiguity notes, pair-recognition notes, completion timestamps, and validation status will be retained for research-method development;
- public research artifacts use reviewer pseudonyms, not names, emails, payment details, tax information, or direct identifiers;
- deidentified results may be reported in aggregate or item-level methodological analyses;
- disagreement, null results, packet failures, suppressed scores, and rejected items will be preserved;
- participation does not imply endorsement of EGC, Qira, the Constructed Subject thesis, or any consciousness claim.

Administrative identity and payment records must be stored separately from the research dataset.

## 7. Withdrawal and incomplete work

- A candidate may decline before queue assignment without penalty.
- After queue assignment but before submission lock, the reviewer may withdraw and request that incomplete review data not be used.
- A locked complete submission cannot be selectively withdrawn because the reviewer dislikes the result after target reveal; legal or ethical deletion obligations remain controlling where applicable.
- Incomplete work is not treated as a scientific submission and is not silently imputed.
- Compensation for partial work is not guaranteed unless withdrawal follows a project-caused technical failure or materially inaccurate workload representation; those cases require documented case review.

## 8. Locking and immutability

A submission is accepted only when:

- all 24 assigned items are present in the assigned order;
- source and queue digests match;
- reviewer pseudonym matches;
- required fields are complete;
- the reviewer affirms no target exposure before lock;
- the submission is locked before any constructor target reveal;
- the submission passes the fail-closed validator.

No score editing is allowed after target reveal. Pre-reveal clerical correction requires a preserved superseding submission and documented reason.

## 9. Replacement and stop rules

A reviewer may be replaced only for a prospectively defined process reason, including:

- decline or non-response before assignment;
- failed eligibility or conflict screening;
- target exposure;
- withdrawal before lock;
- missed deadline after documented reminders;
- corrupted or invalid submission that cannot be repaired before reveal;
- verified independence breach.

A reviewer must not be replaced because their scores are unfavorable, unusually severe, unusually lenient, or inconsistent with constructor expectations.

The tranche stops before target reveal if fewer than three valid independent submissions exist, reviewer independence is compromised, compensation terms changed selectively, or protected information was exposed.

## 10. Permitted and prohibited conclusions

Permitted:

- describing reviewer disagreement and score distributions;
- identifying packets requiring revision, rejection, or further review;
- evaluating whether the provisional rubric appears usable enough for continued pilot development.

Prohibited:

- calling any packet validated solely because three reviewers agree;
- calling the seven-region scale validated;
- inferring hidden intention, consciousness, deception, subjectivity, personhood, or moral status;
- suppressing unfavorable reviewer evidence;
- converting synthetic constructor targets into gold-standard truth.

## 11. Send/no-send rule

No invitation may be sent as a complete participation offer until all of the following are frozen:

- USD 150 fixed compensation;
- seven-day review window;
- workload estimate and technical dry run;
- consent and data-use language;
- conflict and independence screening;
- withdrawal and payment rules;
- secure queue delivery and submission path;
- administrative contact and oversight responsibility;
- an ethics/data-use determination or documented decision path.

If any term changes after the first invitation is sent, the same change must be offered uniformly to all candidates in that sourcing wave, or the wave must restart.
