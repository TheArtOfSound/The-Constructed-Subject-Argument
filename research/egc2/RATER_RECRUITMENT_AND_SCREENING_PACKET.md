# EGC 2.0 Rater Recruitment and Screening Packet

**Status:** Copy-ready draft; compensation, platform, dates, and authorized ethics language must be completed before use  
**Purpose:** Recruit independent raters for the semantic-fidelity measurement pilot  
**Non-claim:** This is a writing-judgment study. Raters are not evaluating consciousness, personality, diagnosis, intelligence, or truthfulness.

## 1. Public recruitment copy

### Title

**Paid Research Raters Needed for a Writing-Meaning Study**

### Description

We are recruiting adults to evaluate how accurately short written responses communicate a documented intended meaning.

You will compare a private intention summary with a written response and score how faithfully the response transmits the central meaning. You will not judge whether you agree with the writer, whether the writing is elegant, or whether the writer appears intelligent.

Selected raters will:

- complete a training and certification exercise;
- score a fixed set of short response pairs;
- provide brief reason codes for some ratings;
- work independently without external AI, search, or outside assistance;
- complete the work on a desktop or laptop.

Expected production workload: **48 scored items after certification**.

## 3. Screening logic

### Automatic disqualification

A candidate is not eligible when any applies:

- under 18;
- not fluent in the study language;
- unable to use the required device/platform;
- refuses the no-assistance rule;
- declines consent;
- helped create or label study items;
- has protected knowledge of monitoring classes or reference scores;
- has an unmanaged conflict of interest;
- fails both construct-comprehension questions.

### Manual review required

Review rather than automatically exclude when a candidate:

- knows a researcher personally;
- reports adjacent professional experience;
- reports prior exposure without clear access to protected materials;
- gives an ambiguous but potentially adequate explanation of fidelity versus polish.

Every manual decision requires:

- reviewer ID;
- decision;
- reason code;
- timestamp;
- second review when the decision is uncertain.

## 6. Prohibited practices

Do not:

- recruit people who created the item bank;
- describe the study as proving AI or human consciousness;
- imply that certification failure reflects low intelligence;
- hide the fact that response time and calibration performance are recorded;
- withhold earned compensation because a candidate failed certification;
- change eligibility rules after seeing candidate characteristics;
- selectively replace raters based on preliminary substantive effects;
- ask for unnecessary medical, financial, immigration, or precise-location information.
