## 3. Expertise and task-fit questions

1. Briefly describe experience relevant to one or more of the following:
   - psychometrics, rater reliability, or constructed-response scoring;
   - semantics, pragmatics, discourse, or psycholinguistics;
   - writing assessment, language assessment, literacy research, or qualitative coding.
2. Have you previously designed, scored, reviewed, or validated a human-rating instrument? If yes, describe the role.
3. Are you comfortable distinguishing preservation of intended meaning from writing quality, length, emotional intensity, ideological agreement, vocabulary sophistication, and rhetorical persuasiveness?
4. Are you able to complete all 24 items independently within the uniform review window?

Expertise supports eligibility but does not confer correctness. Reviewers are independent measurement sources, not authorities whose scores become gold labels automatically.

## 4. Target-exposure and authorship disclosure

Answer yes or no and explain every yes answer.

1. Have you seen any of the 24 anchor packets?
2. Have you seen any constructor target score, admissible score range, rationale, contrast-family label, pair identifier, or protected source mapping?
3. Did you help draft, edit, review, or advise on any anchor, intention map, candidate response, rubric, scoring rule, or review workflow?
4. Have you discussed the item bank or likely scores with Bryan Leonard, another proposed reviewer, Claude, GPT, or any other contributor?
5. Have you seen a public or private document that would allow you to infer the intended answer for a packet?

### Mandatory exclusion

Any confirmed exposure to constructor targets, rationales, protected mappings, or prior item-level scores excludes the candidate from this tranche. Ambiguous exposure is resolved conservatively: no queue is assigned until the exposure risk is documented and cleared.

## 5. Independence and collaboration disclosure

1. Do you know or work closely with any other proposed reviewer?
2. Are you in the same research group, advisor lineage, supervisory relationship, or active coauthor team as another proposed reviewer?
3. Have you agreed to discuss the task with another person while scoring?
4. Can you complete the review without generative AI, search, external scoring tools, or assistance from another person?
5. Can you preserve the assigned item order and avoid comparing answers with another reviewer before submissions lock?

A professional acquaintance does not automatically exclude a candidate. Active collaboration, shared scoring, or coordinated interpretation does.

## 6. Conflict-of-interest disclosure

1. Do you have an authorship, advisory, employment, investment, payment, reputational, or other material interest in a favorable result for EGC, Constructed Subject, Qira, or related products?
2. Would your willingness to participate or remain involved depend on the ratings agreeing with the constructors or supporting publication?
3. Do you have a professional or personal conflict that could reasonably affect your scoring or the appearance of independence?
4. Does your institution require outside-work, consulting, payment, or ethics approval before you can participate?

Payment for the fixed review task is not itself a disqualifying conflict. Outcome-dependent compensation, favorable-result conditions, or undisclosed material interests are disqualifying.

## 7. Required commitments

The candidate must affirm all of the following:

- I will score independently.
- I will not use generative AI, web search, or another person to determine scores.
- I will not share the queue or item content.
- I will not reorder the queue or intentionally revisit earlier items unless the platform explicitly permits it.
- I will report suspected pair recognition and explain the basis.
- I will report an unusable intention map rather than inventing a midpoint score.
- I understand that my complete submission becomes immutable before constructor targets are revealed.
- I understand that disagreement, null results, rejected packets, and ambiguity will be preserved.
- I understand that participation does not endorse EGC, the anchor bank, or any claim about consciousness, hidden intention, subjectivity, or personhood.

## 9. Panel-level eligibility gate

Queue assignment remains blocked unless the eligible panel:

1. contains one reviewer from each required disciplinary lane;
2. spans at least two institutions;
3. contains no target-exposed reviewer;
4. contains no active scoring collaboration;
5. uses identical compensation, consent, data-use, and submission terms;
6. is selected before any item-level score is observed.

## 10. Claims boundary

A completed screening form supports only a documented eligibility decision. It does not establish reviewer accuracy, absence of all bias, adequacy of three reviewers, anchor validity, construct validity, or any EGC conclusion.
